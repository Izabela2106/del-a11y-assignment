# Example WCAG 2.1 Accessibility Audit Table

## 1. Introduction

This audit records how the Championship Day page meets WCAG 2.1 standards. Its purpose is to find barriers for people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, or low-vision settings and provide a fix.
